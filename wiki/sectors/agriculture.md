# Agriculture — Pathway Index

AI deployments in the agriculture sector typically address the same structural problem: institutional agricultural knowledge (research outputs, scheme databases, weather data, market prices) exists but has no route to the farmer in the field at the cadence farming decisions require. The human extension system cannot scale to deliver personalised, timely advice to the millions of smallholder farmers who need it. What makes this sector distinctive for AI deployment is the combination of real-time data dependency (weather, pest alerts, market prices change daily), deep language diversity (farmers in most countries speak dozens of regional languages), and extreme access constraint (target users may have only a basic phone, intermittent connectivity, and no literacy in a dominant language). The most successful deployments are built voice-first, draw on federated institutional data rather than centralising it, and position AI as the delivery interface for institutional knowledge rather than as the knowledge authority itself.

## Pathways in this sector

### [MahaVistaar](../pathways/mahavistaar.md)
**Geography:** Maharashtra, India | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active (Scaling)
The founding OAN deployment: built without a playbook in nine months; produced 342,000+ unique users and 1.67M+ questions answered. Essential reading for any government adopter planning a state-level agricultural AI advisory system.

### [Bharat-VISTAAR](../pathways/bharat-vistaar.md)
**Geography:** India (national) | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active (Pilot)
India's national agricultural DPI — the common rail connecting state and cooperative nodes. Useful to adopters asking how a national government can amplify what states have already built rather than replacing it. Rs. 150 crore Union Budget allocation; launched February 2026.

### [Amul Sarlaben](../pathways/amul-sarlaben.md)
**Geography:** Gujarat, India | **Actor type:** Cooperative | **Status:** Active (Scaling)
The world's largest dairy cooperative placing personalised AI advisory in the hands of 3.6 million women producers. Deployed in three weeks by adapting MahaVistaar's architecture. Useful to cooperative adopters with pre-existing member data infrastructure.

### [Bihar Krishi](../pathways/bihar-krishi.md)
**Geography:** Bihar, India | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active (Scaling)
Independent state agricultural platform built under Bihar's 4th Agriculture Roadmap, with 850,000+ registered farmers, 15,000+ trained extension workers, and two national awards. Demonstrates that a state government can build a coherent platform independently and subsequently connect to a national layer.

### [Ethiopia ATI](../pathways/ethiopia-ati.md)
**Geography:** Ethiopia (national) | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active (Pilot)
First OAN deployment outside India; three months from commitment to launch. Useful to non-Indian national governments asking how to adapt the OAN architecture to a new country context.
