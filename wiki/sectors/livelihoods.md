# Livelihoods — Pathway Index

AI and digital deployments in the livelihoods sector typically address employment discovery failures: youth, women, and employers are geographically proximate but digitally invisible to each other. The challenge is not a shortage of jobs or workers but a shortage of credible, timely, searchable discovery infrastructure. What makes this sector distinctive is the multi-sided nature of the problem (both job seekers and employers must be made discoverable simultaneously), the critical role of verified credibility (unverified profiles cause hiring failures and erode trust), and the importance of sustainability through local ecosystem activation rather than government-only delivery. AI inference plays a less central role in the documented livelihoods deployment than in agricultural advisory — the value is in digital mapping and continuous discovery, not in AI-generated recommendations.

**Geography:** Ghaziabad (UP) and Dharwad (Karnataka), India | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active (Scaling)
A district-level employment discovery approach that reduced employer hiring costs approximately 10× and cut time-to-fill from weeks to days. Seeded by EkStep Foundation; piloted in two districts with a published playbook for replication. Useful to district-level government adopters seeking to build a continuous employment exchange from existing institutional data.
