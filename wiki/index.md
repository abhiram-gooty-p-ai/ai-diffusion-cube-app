# AI Diffusion Cube — Index

## Pathways

- [MahaVistaar](pathways/mahavistaar.md) — Maharashtra state government agricultural AI advisory; anchor OAN deployment; 342K+ users, 1.67M+ questions answered, 17 lakh daily proactive alerts; nine months pioneer build
- [Bharat-VISTAAR](pathways/bharat-vistaar.md) — India's national agricultural DPI; common rail connecting state/cooperative nodes; Rs. 150 crore allocation; launched February 2026
- [Amul Sarlaben](pathways/amul-sarlaben.md) — Amul cooperative dairy AI for 3.6M women producers in Gujarat; deployed in three weeks by adapting MahaVistaar architecture
- [Bihar Krishi](pathways/bihar-krishi.md) — Bihar state agricultural platform; 850K+ farmers, 38 districts, 15K+ extension workers trained; built independently then connected to national layer
- [Ethiopia ATI](pathways/ethiopia-ati.md) — First international OAN deployment; national agricultural advisory for 30M Ethiopian farmers; three months from commitment to launch
- [Blue Dots](pathways/blue-dots.md) — District livelihoods employment discovery; Ghaziabad and Dharwad pilots; 10× employer hiring cost reduction; continuous matching replacing episodic job fairs
- [Project Astitva](pathways/project-astitva.md) — Nandurbar district participatory language AI; tribal community speech data for Bhili and other languages; feeds into MahaVistaar voice capability
