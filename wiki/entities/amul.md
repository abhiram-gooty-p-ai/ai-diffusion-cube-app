# Amul (Gujarat Cooperative Milk Marketing Federation)

**Type:** Organisation
**Role:** Deployer (cooperative)
**Geography:** Gujarat, India (18,500+ villages)
**Contact or reference:** Not publicly documented in source material

## What they are

Amul is the world's largest dairy cooperative, processing 3.5 crore litres of milk daily from 3.6 million milk producers — predominantly women — across 18,500+ villages in Gujarat. Amul maintains one of the most extensive cooperative data infrastructures in the world: 2 billion milk procurement transactions, records on 30 million cattle each with a unique ID tracking feed, health, treatment, and milking history, and 1,200+ veterinary doctors contributing records annually, accumulated over 50 years of cooperative operations. Amul is relevant to AI diffusion as the fastest documented OAN deployment (three weeks from commitment to live system) and as the primary example of a private sector cooperative adding an AI advisory layer to a pre-existing data infrastructure — without requiring the institutional data-sharing negotiations that government deployments face.

## When this entity is relevant to an adopter

An adopter should study Amul when: (1) asking how a cooperative or membership organisation with deep existing transaction data can add an AI advisory layer for its members; (2) evaluating how institutional trust built over decades compresses AI deployment timelines; or (3) designing individual-level rather than population-level advisory (Amul's system advises based on the specific animal's records, not generic livestock guidance).
