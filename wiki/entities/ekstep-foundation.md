# EkStep Foundation

**Type:** Organisation
**Role:** Network orchestrator / Technical partner / Enabler
**Geography:** India (national) with international reach (Ethiopia)
**Contact or reference:** Not publicly documented in source material

## What they are

EkStep Foundation is a non-profit technology organisation that built and maintains the OpenAgriNet (OAN) digital public goods architecture and holds the network orchestrator role across all OAN agricultural AI deployments. EkStep's role is to maintain the shared open-source infrastructure — the OAN DPG layer, governance frameworks, deployment playbooks, and failure mode library — so that each new deployment can adapt rather than rebuild from scratch. In the livelihoods sector, EkStep also seeded the Blue Dots employment discovery approach. EkStep is relevant to AI diffusion because it occupies the orchestrator position: not the government deployer, not the technology vendor, but the organisation that holds the network together and ensures that each deployment compounds the knowledge available to the next.

## When this entity is relevant to an adopter

An adopter should engage EkStep Foundation when: (1) considering an agricultural AI advisory deployment on the OAN pathway and needing access to the DPG layer, governance frameworks, deployment playbooks, or ecosystem maps; (2) seeking to connect an existing state platform to the Bharat-VISTAAR national layer; (3) exploring the Blue Dots approach for a livelihoods context; or (4) evaluating whether to build from scratch or adapt existing OAN architecture — EkStep can advise on what adaptation requires versus what is already built.
