# Agricultural Transformation Institute (ATI), Ethiopia

**Type:** Organisation
**Role:** National transformation institute / Lead deployer
**Geography:** Ethiopia (national)
**Contact or reference:** Not publicly documented in source material

## What they are

The Agricultural Transformation Institute (ATI) is Ethiopia's national institution for agricultural system transformation, and the lead organisation for Ethiopia's first OAN agricultural AI advisory deployment. ATI's role in this deployment is as national integrator: aligning the Ministry of Agriculture, international development partners, and technology enablers around a single national architecture rather than allowing fragmented parallel efforts. ATI operates within Ethiopia's Digital Agriculture Roadmap 2025–2032, which identifies 22 prioritised use cases across six solution areas. Ethiopia's deployment is the first OAN implementation outside India, established three months from commitment to launch (February 2026), and is positioned by ATI as a vehicle for Ethiopia to become a regional leader in digital agriculture across Sub-Saharan Africa.

An adopter outside India should engage ATI when: (1) seeking a peer deployer who has navigated the OAN pathway adaptation for a non-Indian context; (2) asking how a national transformation institute prevents fragmented parallel efforts from multiple government departments and development partners; or (3) exploring Sub-Saharan African agricultural AI deployment experience, particularly in the context of climate intelligence integration.
