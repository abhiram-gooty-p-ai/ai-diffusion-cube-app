# Bhashini / AI4Bharat

**Type:** System / Organisation
**Role:** Language infrastructure provider
**Geography:** India (national)
**Contact or reference:** Not publicly documented in source material

## What they are

Bhashini is India's national language AI platform, built by AI4Bharat, providing multilingual automatic speech recognition (ASR) and text-to-speech (TTS) capabilities across Indian languages. In the context of agricultural AI deployments, Bhashini provides the voice pipeline that enables a farmer to speak in Marathi, Hindi, Bhili, or Gujarati and receive a spoken response in the same language — making voice-first AI accessible to populations with low literacy and no smartphone. AI4Bharat is the research organisation that builds the underlying language models; Bhashini is the platform through which they are accessible to government and civil society deployers. Bhashini also hosts community-generated language data repositories (such as the Dehwali Bhili repository from Project Astitva) as open resources.

## Deployments they are associated with

- [MahaVistaar](../pathways/mahavistaar.md) — ASR/TTS for Marathi, Hindi, Bhili, and English voice interface
- [Amul Sarlaben](../pathways/amul-sarlaben.md) — Gujarati voice access; planned expansion to 20 Indian languages
- [Project Astitva](../pathways/project-astitva.md) — Hosting infrastructure for the Dehwali Bhili community language repository; participant in the Tribaldaan conclave

## When this entity is relevant to an adopter

An adopter should engage Bhashini/AI4Bharat when: (1) designing a voice-first AI system for a multilingual Indian population; (2) needing ASR or TTS capabilities for regional Indian languages beyond Hindi and English; (3) seeking to host a community-generated language dataset as an open resource; or (4) assessing whether language coverage exists for a specific Indian language or dialect before committing to a voice-first design.
