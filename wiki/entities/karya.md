# Karya

**Type:** Organisation
**Role:** Ethical dataset creator / Community data collection partner
**Geography:** India
**Contact or reference:** Not publicly documented in source material

## What they are

Karya is a social enterprise that specialises in creating ethical datasets for underserved communities. Its model: community members are compensated for contributing voice data, speech recordings, and other training data for AI systems in their own languages. Karya's methodology combines consent-based data collection, community compensation, and quality protocols designed for low-literacy and low-resource language contexts. In the OAN agricultural AI ecosystem, Karya contributed voice data that enabled Bhashini ASR/TTS for tribal languages — specifically through the Project Astitva collaboration with Nandurbar District Administration. Karya's relevance to AI diffusion is its demonstration that building language AI capabilities for underserved communities can be both ethically structured and practically effective within a month-long intensive effort.

## Deployments they are associated with

- [MahaVistaar](../pathways/mahavistaar.md) — Voice data contributor (Bhili and other language data enabling multilingual voice access)
- [Project Astitva](../pathways/project-astitva.md) — Primary implementation partner for community speech data collection in Nandurbar

## When this entity is relevant to an adopter

An adopter should engage Karya when: (1) building voice AI capabilities for low-resource languages or tribal language communities; (2) needing a methodology for ethical, compensated community data collection that produces quality training data; or (3) designing a participatory AI data collection initiative and needing expertise in consent design, community compensation, and quality protocols for underserved populations.
