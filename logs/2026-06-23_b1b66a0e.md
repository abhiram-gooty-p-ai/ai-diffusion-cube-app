Good — then it's worth being honest about where this checklist will be *used* versus where it will be *ignored*, because the questions people skip are the ones that kill deployments. Let me give you the full set, organised so an adopter can actually work through it. Drawn from MahaVistaar, Bihar Krishi, Amul, Ethiopia, and Blue Dots.

**Where do I start? (Problem)**
- Can you name the specific person whose life gets better, and exactly how? More than half of builders can't articulate their own problem without help.
- Did the *user* validate the problem, or just the deployer? Usage volume is the real test — MahaVistaar's 205,000 monthly queries confirmed the framing was right.
- Are your success metrics measuring system activity (calls, downloads) or actual outcomes (yield, income, placements)? Define the outcome metric *before* launch, even if it's harder to count — otherwise the system looks successful by its own measures while delivering less.
- Does the data already exist to be *connected*, or are you waiting to *collect* it? Deployments that wait for clean data often never start.

**What do I build with? (Architecture)**
- Can you swap the AI model without rebuilding the system? Make the architecture decision before the model decision.
- Are you connecting to data where it lives, or centralising it? Centralising triggers sovereignty disputes and stops institutional partners from participating.
- What's your exit path if a vendor fails or changes terms?
- What catches a wrong or harmful output *before* it reaches the user — not after a complaint?
- For your actual users — voice, language, literacy, connectivity — does the system reach the hardest-to-reach person, or just the median one?

**What kind of undertaking is this? (Institution)**
- Who can say "this runs in my name"?
- Project or permanent capability — and does the framing match?
- Does it survive the champion leaving? Is it embedded in a budget line or roadmap, not one person's enthusiasm?
- Does any single authority exist over all the departments that must cooperate?

**Who does what? (Ecosystem)**
- Is one person explicitly assigned and resourced to keep partners aligned?
- Have you designed each partner's *own* incentive to stay — or are you relying on shared mission? Goodwill decays.
- Where do partners' mandates conflict, and have you surfaced that before assembling them?

**How do my people absorb this? (Workforce)**
- Is training part of the deployment, or bolted on after? Bihar trained 15,000+ extension workers as part of the rollout, not at the end.
- Are you training for compliance (follow the workflow) or capability (understand what the AI can't do, so they know when to override or escalate)?
- Watch both failure directions: do staff reflexively rubber-stamp the AI, *or* reflexively override it? Both kill the human judgment the design depends on.
- Twelve-month test: can the frontline worker still function if the system goes down?

**What makes it last? (Operating Model)**
- Who owns steady-state operations after the pilot, and is that decided *before* the pilot starts?
- When the initial/grant funding ends, what's the domestic funding line? If you can't name it, the deployment is already dead even if it looks alive.
- Add one capability at a time (plus-one), not ten. Amul went advisory → insemination booking → microcredit, each building on proven readiness.
- Did you sequence through small pilots, or stage a vanity launch?
