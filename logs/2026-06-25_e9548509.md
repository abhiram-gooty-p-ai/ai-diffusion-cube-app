**Segment 2 (25 min) — The data problem (the hardest part)**
This deserves the most time because it's where the effort lives or dies. Walk through:

Lighter touch, but decide: component architecture (ASR + LLM + TTS) rather than end-to-end speech-to-speech, because you'll need domain control and guardrails. And confirm whether your use case needs outbound calling (system calls citizen) as well as inbound — because that shapes the architecture and is expensive to retrofit later.
