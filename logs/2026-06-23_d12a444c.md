**Keeping the advisory current.** Investment policy is a moving target — incentive thresholds change, new schemes get notified, clearance rules get amended. The failure pattern here is the *staleness cascade*: the bot keeps confidently giving last quarter's incentive rate or a superseded eligibility rule, and the Udyami Mitra passes it to an investor. The architectural answer that recurs across these deployments is to connect to departmental sources live at query time rather than loading a static snapshot the bot answers from. Worth asking yourself: which of your data changes fast (notifications, rates, scheme status) versus slow (process steps), and is your design connecting to the fast-changing ones in real time?

**The institution behind it — is this a project or a capability?** This is the quiet killer. If it's framed as a one-time build, it gets a budget, a pilot, a report, and then drifts when the person driving it moves. For you: who owns this in steady state once the build team moves on? And if the official championing it today gets transferred, does it survive?
