### Assistant

Good place to start — and the honest answer is that the most useful first step isn't technical at all. Before we talk about what to build, I'd like to understand the complaints themselves, because that shapes everything that follows.

Let me ask one thing at a time.

When you say "the complaints I receive" — who is sending them, and what are they typically about? For example: are these citizens complaining about a service not working, businesses raising grievances, internal staff escalations, something else?

I ask because "addressing complaints" can mean very different things — routing them to the right desk faster, answering the person who complained, spotting patterns across many complaints, or actually resolving the underlying issue. Each of those is a different problem, and they lead to different systems.

So: who complains to you, and what do they complain about?
